IP65 covers dust and water jets, not continuous rain at an angle or condensation forming inside the housing.

For outdoor mounting, keep the cable entry pointing down, leave a drip loop, and consider a sun/rain shield. If condensation is likely, a breather element is more effective than a higher IP number.
