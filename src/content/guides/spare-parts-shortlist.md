Most spare lists are a column of model codes. Enough to reorder, not enough to act at 2 a.m.

## Record the mounting, not just the model
Thread, insertion depth and clearance. Without them the replacement arrives and does not fit the bracket.

## Record the function
Write "dosing pump outlet, 0–1.6 MPa" next to the code. Six months later nobody remembers which line the spare belongs to.

## Record the revision
A batch or date tells you whether the spare matches the unit in service after a supplier changes a component.

## Write the replacement rule
"Replace when zero drift exceeds 0.5% FS" is a rule. "Replace when it looks bad" is a guess.
