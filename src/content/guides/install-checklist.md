Most field failures in humid locations are installation issues, not product issues.

## Before mounting
Check that the seal is seated and undamaged. A twisted O-ring still tightens, and still leaks.

## Cable entry direction
Point the entry downward. An upward entry collects water even with a sealed gland.

## Service loop
Leave a drip loop below the entry so water runs off the cable instead of into the gland.

## After the first week
Re-torque the connection and inspect the seal. Thermal cycling loosens joints in the first days.

## What to record
Ambient range, medium and installation date — future troubleshooting starts from these three values.
